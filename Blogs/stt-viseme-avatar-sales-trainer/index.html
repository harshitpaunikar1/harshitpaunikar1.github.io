STT + Viseme Avatar Sales Trainer

A sales training platform where the user speaks, the system transcribes in real time, evaluates tone and content, generates coaching feedback, and drives a lip-synced avatar using viseme events. Built for sales enablement, call-center coaching, language practice, and interview rehearsal -- contexts where static LMS content is too passive to build real conversational skill.

Primary Use

A new salesperson practices selling a SaaS product. The avatar says that a competitor is cheaper, the salesperson responds verbally, and the system evaluates whether the objection was handled with value, proof, and confidence.

System Built

A real-time coaching loop combining STT, evaluation, TTS, viseme animation, and training analytics.

Critical Control

Consent before recording

Business Outcome

Scalable sales coaching -- new team members practice anytime, managers track improvement through dashboards, and the feedback loop no longer depends on manager availability.

Implementation Lens: Defined the trainer as a real-time conversation loop rather than a batch transcript analyzer so timing, interruption, and avatar feedback all matter. Mapped the media chain across STT, response evaluation, TTS, viseme streaming, and scorecard generation.

Section I
Operating Problem

Section I · Why this system exists as an operating product rather than a feature demo

This project solved a recurring training problem: sales teams practice scripts, but they rarely receive objective feedback on tone, clarity, objection handling, and confidence. Managers cannot listen to every practice call, and new salespeople often improve slowly because feedback arrives too late.

The solution turned sales training into an interactive avatar-based simulation. The user speaks to a virtual customer, speech-to-text converts the response, the AI evaluates the answer, and the avatar responds naturally using speech synthesis with viseme-based lip movement.

The system was designed not as a talking avatar demo but as a repeatable coaching loop. Every session generated a scorecard covering opening quality, objection handling, product understanding, confidence, filler words, and closing strength.

Value delivered. Scalable, on-demand sales coaching. New team members practice anytime without waiting for manager availability, and managers track improvement over time through structured dashboards.

Section IV
Delivery Path

Section IV · Delivery discipline decides whether the first version proves the right thing

Difficulty Faced

The hardest challenge was synchronization. Voice capture, transcription, AI response, avatar animation, and scoring all had to feel connected in real time. The solution: split the pipeline into a real-time conversation layer (optimized for responsiveness) and a post-session deep-analysis layer (optimized for accuracy).

The core tension: keeping transcript latency, avatar lip-sync, and coaching quality aligned closely enough that the simulation feels believable.

Client Coordination Method

The project was shaped through sales-manager workshops where real objections, role-play scripts, and examples of strong versus weak responses were collected. That allowed the avatar to behave like a realistic buyer rather than a generic chatbot.

Best fit: Voice AI roles, Real-time systems roles, EdTech roles

  • Added consent, retention controls, accent bias testing, and coach access boundaries because voice systems create immediate trust risk.
  • Positioned the product as per-seat training SaaS with LMS and CRM tie-ins rather than a pure media demo.
Section V
Decision Boundary

Section V · The most important design choice is usually the boundary that fails first

This project illustrates multimodal system design where the value comes from coordination between media pipelines, not from any single model alone.

The trainer works best when scoring is attached to specific moments in the conversation, not an abstract overall grade at the end.

Biggest Surprise

Users react strongly to lip-sync quality. Small viseme lag makes the entire coaching layer feel less trustworthy.

Next Step

The next version should add interruption handling and scenario branching so the coach can respond to hesitation, objections, and silence more naturally.
